1. locatix.admin 
   - /locatix reload: locatix.admin.reload
   - /locatix resetconfig: locatix.admin.resetconfig
   - /locatix get <player>: locatix.admin.get
   - /locatix serverlang <lang>: locatix.admin.serverlang
   - /locatix adminpanel: locatix.admin.panel
   - teleport: locatix.admin.teleport
   - see admin commands: locatix.admin.help

2. locatix.player
   - /locatix help: locatix.player.help
   - /locatix coordinates: locatix.player.location
   - /locatix convert <nether/world>: locatix.player.convert
   - /locatix lastdeath: locatix.player.lastdeath
   - /locatix share <player>: locatix.player.share
   - /locatix lang <lang>: locatix.player.lang

3. PAPI
   - %locatix_x% - Player X Location
   - %locatix_y% - Player Y Location
   - %locatix_z% - Player Z Location
   - %locatix_nether_coords% - Change overworld location to nether
   - %locatix_overworld_coords% - Change nether location to overworld
   - %locatix_lastdeath_coords% - Last death XYZ Location
   - %locatix_lastdeath_world% - Last death world
